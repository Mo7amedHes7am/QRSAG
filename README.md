# 📱 QR Scanner & Generator (Flutter)

A powerful, modern **QR Scanner & Generator** app built with **Flutter**, supporting **30+ languages**, multiple QR types, history tracking, and customizable settings.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🔳 QR Generation Types
Supports generating QR codes for:

- 📶 WiFi
- 🌐 Website URL
- 📝 Text
- ❌ X (Twitter)
- 💬 WhatsApp
- 👤 Contact (vCard)
- 📞 Phone
- 📸 Instagram
- 📧 Email

> 🚀 Upcoming:
- 💳 Visa QR
- 🎫 Event QR
- 🏢 Business QR

---

### 📷 QR Scanner
- Fast and accurate scanning
- Supports all standard QR formats
- Instant result parsing

---

### 🗂️ History System
- 📥 Scan History (stored separately)
- 📤 Generated QR History (stored separately)
- Local persistent storage using Hive
- Offline access

---

### ⚙️ Settings
- 🔔 Sound toggle (scan feedback)
- 📳 Vibration toggle
- 🌍 Change language
- 🎨 Theme customization
- 📞 Contact support
- 🔒 Privacy policy

---

## 🌍 Supported Languages

English (en), Chinese (zh), Spanish (es), Arabic (ar), Hindi (hi), Bengali (bn), Portuguese (pt), Russian (ru), French (fr), German (de), Japanese (ja), Punjabi (pa), Marathi (mr), Telugu (te), Vietnamese (vi), Turkish (tr), Tamil (ta), Korean (ko), Persian (fa), Urdu (ur), Italian (it), Thai (th), Polish (pl), Ukrainian (uk), Dutch (nl), Romanian (ro), Indonesian (id), Malay (ms), Swahili (sw), Greek (el)

---

## 🏗️ Tech Stack

- Flutter 3.44.7 (Stable)
- Dart 3.12.2
- State Management: Cubit (Bloc)
- Local Storage: Hive
- Clean Architecture
